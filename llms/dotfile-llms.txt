- [Render a document template for a case](https://docs.dotfile.com/reference/case-render-document-template.md): Render a `case_report` document template for a case and get the PDF back in the response. The latest published version of the template is used. No request body is needed. Unlike `POST /cases/{id}/generate-report`, which queues the full case report and delivers it through the `CaseReport.Generated` webhook, this endpoint answers synchronously and renders the template configured in your workspace.  Rendering goes through a third party, so this endpoint can also answer `CASE_REPORT_RENDER_FAILED`: a `400` carrying the reason when the template itself cannot be rendered, or a `502` when the rendering service is unavailable. Retrying only helps the `502`.  ---  #### See also   Learn more about [Cases](./cases-guide)
